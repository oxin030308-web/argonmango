# POLAR SHIFT 3D — a Scratch game

> *Opposites attract. Opposites kill.*

You are a magnetic core. Every enemy is **cyan** or **magenta**, and so are you.

| Touches you | Result |
|---|---|
| **Same** color | you **absorb** it (points + combo) |
| **Opposite** color | it **absorbs you**: game over |

The new mechanic is that magnetism moves everything:

- **Opposite** colors get **pulled straight at you**.
- **Same** colors get **pushed away** and circle at a distance.

You can't chase down the enemies you're allowed to eat, because they flee. So you let the killers rush in and **flip your polarity at the last second**. Their own momentum carries them into you and you absorb them. Do it within about 0.4 s of flipping and it's a **CLUTCH** (double points, extra energy).

Flipping costs energy (the bar at the top holds 3 flips). Absorbing enemies refills it, so a good chain keeps you going and spamming flips leaves you defenseless.

## Controls
| | Keyboard | Touch / mouse |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Arrows / WASD | drag the on-screen stick (bottom-left) |
| Flip | Space | tap the FLIP button, or tap/click anywhere else |
| Start / retry | Space | tap |

The FLIP button shows the color you'll flip *to*, and reads **LOW** when you don't have enough energy.
Scratch only reads one touch at a time, so on a phone you let go of the stick to flip.

## 3D
The game runs on a 3D world plane, and everything is projected through a perspective camera:
- The arena backdrop is pre-rendered with the same camera, so the floor grid, glowing walls and corner pylons line up exactly with the sprites.
- Enemies are flat-shaded 3D meshes (octahedrons, hex prisms, darts), each rendered from 8 angles so they spin or turn to face where they're heading.
- Objects hover and bob, cast shadows, and are drawn in front of or behind each other by depth. Your core lights up the floor in its color.
- Enemies drop in from the sky, and sparks arc up and bounce off the floor.

## Features
- Endless waves that get faster and denser
- **Prisms** (hexagons, wave 3+): flicker, then swap color
- **Hunters** (darts, wave 4+): fast and aggressive
- Combo multiplier: the pop sound rises in pitch as your chain grows
- Crackling magnetic tethers to incoming killers, a comet trail, shockwave rings, particle bursts, 7-segment neon HUD
- Procedurally generated synthwave soundtrack and sound effects
- Best score tracking

## Putting it on Scratch
1. Open your project in the Scratch editor.
2. **File → Load from your computer** → pick `PolarShift.sb3`.
3. **File → Save now**, then share.

## Rebuilding
All art, audio and code are generated by script:

```
pip install numpy
python3 build.py      # writes PolarShift.sb3
```

- `build.py`: the game logic, written with a small Python DSL
- `dsl.py`: compiles that DSL into Scratch 3 blocks
- `art3d.py`: the camera projection, a mesh-to-SVG renderer, the 3D arena and the touch controls
- `assets.py`: 2D neon art (HUD digits, text cards) and synthesized WAV audio
